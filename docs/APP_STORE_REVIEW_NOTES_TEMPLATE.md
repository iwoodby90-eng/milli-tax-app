# App Store Review Notes — Milli

## Demo access

Provide a dedicated review account with seeded, non-sensitive demo data. Do not use a personal account.

## Core purpose

Milli helps independent workers estimate and reserve taxes, track business mileage, organize deductible expenses, and understand their financial position. Milli is not a bank, tax authority, or investment adviser unless and until the applicable regulated partner relationship is active and disclosed in the submitted build.

## Background location

Background location is optional and used only when the user enables automatic mileage tracking. The user can stop tracking at any time from the Mileage screen. Location data is used to detect and record potential business drives for mileage records.

## In-app purchases

Digital subscription access is offered through Apple In-App Purchase. The submitted build includes purchase restoration and server-side entitlement verification.

## Review path

1. Sign in with the review account.
2. Open Home to view the dashboard.
3. Open Mileage and start a manual trip.
4. Open Milli Tax Vault to inspect tax-reserve records.
5. Open Settings > Subscription to inspect purchase and restore controls.
6. Open Settings > Privacy to find account deletion and data-export controls.
