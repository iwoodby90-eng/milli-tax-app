Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) Privacy Notice

Effective Date · February 18, 2026

This notice is provided by Milli Tax Inc. (“Milli,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a Michigan corporation, in accordance with the federal Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (15 U.S.C. § 6801 et seq.) and its implementing regulations. It describes what nonpublic personal financial information we collect about you, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have about our sharing practices.

1. What financial information we collect

In order to provide the Milli service — automatic tax slicing, mileage tracking, and wealth-building tools — we collect the following categories of nonpublic personal information:

  • From you directly: name, contact details, tax filing status, Social Security or Taxpayer Identification Number (only when required for tax filing services), income sources, dependents, and household information.
  • From your gig platforms and financial institutions (with your consent, through Plaid or similar aggregators): account balances, transaction history, deposit and payout records, and account identifiers.
  • From your device (with permission): background GPS location for mileage logs, timestamps, and device identifiers.
  • Generated by our systems: Autopilot allocation records, Milli Tax Vault™ balances, tax estimates, receipts, and audit trails.

2. How we use your financial information

We use nonpublic personal information solely to operate, secure, and improve the Service, including to:

  • Calculate estimated federal, state, and self-employment tax obligations against current IRS brackets.
  • Allocate every payout into tax, retirement, investing, and available-to-spend buckets on your behalf.
  • Log business, personal, medical, charitable, and commute mileage in an IRS-compliant format.
  • Detect fraud, unauthorized access, or misuse of accounts.
  • Provide customer support you request from us.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and audit obligations.

3. Whom we share information with

Milli does not sell your nonpublic personal information. We share it only in the limited circumstances below and only to the extent necessary for those purposes:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under written contracts requiring confidentiality and GLBA-equivalent safeguards — for example, our cloud infrastructure, Plaid (bank aggregation), Stripe (billing), and, when activated, our approved banking partners.
  • Affiliates of Milli Tax Inc. that operate under the same privacy program.
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by valid legal process, subpoena, or to protect our rights and safety.
  • You or your authorized representative — including tax preparers or CPAs you explicitly designate.
  • Successors in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, subject to continued protection of your data.

We do not share your nonpublic personal information with unaffiliated third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. Your right to opt out

Because we do not share nonpublic personal information with unaffiliated third parties for marketing, no federal opt-out is required. If we ever propose to change this practice, we will notify you in advance and provide a clear opt-out choice before any such sharing takes place.

You may at any time:

  • Disconnect a linked financial account inside the Milli app.
  • Revoke background location permission from your device settings.
  • Delete your Milli account, which triggers deletion of associated financial data within the timeframes described in our Privacy Policy, subject to legal retention requirements.

5. How we protect your information

Milli maintains an information security program with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of your nonpublic personal information. Details of our safeguards, including encryption, access controls, monitoring, and incident response, are summarized on our Security page. All personnel with access to customer data are bound by confidentiality obligations and receive periodic security training.

6. Former customers

If your relationship with Milli ends, we will continue to treat your nonpublic personal information under this notice and our Privacy Policy for as long as we retain it under our records-retention schedule and applicable law.

7. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The current version will always be available at drivemilli.com/glba. Material changes will be communicated in-app and, where possible, by email at least thirty (30) days before they take effect.
