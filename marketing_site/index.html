// Command Center

Your financial cockpit. Three engines. Zero effort.

01 · TAX VAULT™

Automatic Tax Slicing

The moment a payout lands, your Command Center intercepts. Federal, state, and self-employment obligations are computed in real time against live IRS brackets — then locked in your Tax Vault™. No thinking. No quarterly panic. Taxes stay ready, always.

  • Real-time federal + state + SE tax — computed against current IRS brackets
  • Funds auto-allocated to your Milli Tax Vault™ the instant a payout clears
  • Immutable, SHA-256 hashed receipt filed for every transaction — audit-proof by default
  • Quarterly countdown with exact amounts due — never miss an estimated payment
02 · MILEAGE INTELLIGENCE

Autonomous Trip Tracking

Background GPS silently tracks every mile you drive for work. No buttons. No manual entry. Milli auto-detects gig trips, classifies them, and logs IRS-compliant deductions that reduce your tax burden with zero effort.

  • Battery-optimized background location — silent and accurate like Waze
  • Every mile logged at the current IRS standard rate for business use
  • One-tap classification: Business · Personal · Medical · Charitable · Commute
  • Deductions feed directly into your Command Center tax calculations
03 · WEALTH ENGINE

Compound Your Independence

Solo 401(k) tracking, tax-advantaged investing guidance, and autopilot contributions — all inside your Command Center. Milli shows exactly how much you can shelter from taxes and projects how it compounds over decades.

  • Solo 401(k), Traditional & Roth IRA, SEP IRA — unified in one cockpit
  • Autopilot % contributions from every payout into retirement + investing + savings
  • Real-time growth projections — see your wealth trajectory over 10, 20, 30 years
  • Tax shelter optimization: maximize write-offs and deductions automatically
