By accessing or using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account registration**: name, email address, phone number, and password.
- **Identity verification for future Milli Elite Card enrollment**: if production card enrollment becomes available, the approved card program may require legal name, date of birth, tax-identification information, residential address, phone number, email address, and other compliance information. Milli will update the production enrollment flow and this policy before collecting information that is not already described here.
- **Gig platform credentials**: information you provide to connect your earnings platforms through our integrations.
- **Support communications**: messages, feedback, or requests you send to us.

### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

- **Usage data**: features accessed, screens viewed, actions taken within the app, and session duration.
- **Device information**: device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and IP address.
- **Log data**: error logs, crash reports, and diagnostic information.

### 2.3 Information We Collect Through Third-Party Integrations

- **Plaid**: when you link an external bank account, Plaid collects and transmits your bank account and routing numbers, account balances, and transaction history to enable payout routing and reserve verification. Your use of Plaid is also governed by Plaid's Privacy Policy (https://plaid.com/legal).
- **Column Bank**: Column Bank ("Column") is Milli's intended banking, ACH, and card-infrastructure partner. In the current development environment, Milli uses Column Sandbox with synthetic test identities only. If production banking or card enrollment becomes available, Column may collect information necessary for applicable identity, banking, card-program, fraud, and anti-money-laundering requirements. Your use of any live Column-provided product will also be governed by the applicable Column agreements and privacy disclosures.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and maintain your Milli account.
- Provide, operate, and improve the Service, including Tax Vault, Autopilot, Milli-Cents, Milli AI, and related features.
- Route payouts and manage reserve allocations through Column Bank's ACH infrastructure.
- Support Milli Elite Card enrollment and card issuance through Column if the production card program is approved and launched.
- Connect and verify your external bank accounts through Plaid.
- Calculate your Tax Ready Score and provide financial insights.
- Send transactional notifications, account alerts, and service updates.
- Respond to support requests.
- Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud and unauthorized activity.
- Comply with applicable laws and regulatory obligations.
- Analyze usage trends to improve product experience.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 4. Data Handling for Milli Elite Card Enrollment

The production Milli Elite Card enrollment flow is not yet live. Current Column Sandbox testing uses synthetic test identities generated by Milli's backend; it does not require a user's real SSN, date of birth, or residential address.

Before any production card enrollment is enabled, Milli will implement the approved Column identity/compliance flow and update this policy to describe the final required fields, retention rules, disclosures, and cardholder agreements. Milli's architecture is designed so provider credentials and regulated card credentials remain server-side; the mobile app will not be treated as a financial authority.

Milli does not expose full card PAN or CVV values through its ordinary backend APIs. Any future credential-reveal flow must use the provider's approved short-lived reveal mechanism and applicable PCI controls.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When you close your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 90 days, except where longer retention is required by applicable law or regulation (including banking and financial recordkeeping requirements applicable to Column Bank and other regulated providers).

The current Column Sandbox card proof does not collect real customer SSNs or government-issued identity documents. Production identity-data retention terms will be disclosed before live card enrollment is enabled.

## 7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including:

- Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest.
- Server-authoritative architecture: financial authority and provider credentials are held server-side only; the mobile client holds no financial secrets.
- Least-privilege access controls and partner boundary separation.
- Operational monitoring and incident response procedures.

No security system is impenetrable. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.
