## 4. The Service — Description and Scope

### 4.1 Core Features (Live)

The following features are currently available within the Service:

- **Milli Home**: unified dashboard displaying available cash, payout history, Tax Vault balance, Tax Ready Score, and financial timeline.
- **Tax Vault**: a savings reserve account, provided through Column Bank (N.A.), for setting aside estimated tax obligations on each payout.
- **Autopilot**: set-once payout routing engine that automatically allocates each incoming payout across tax reserve, retirement, investing, and savings based on your configured rules.
- **Milli-Cents**: pre-accept offer evaluator that calculates true per-mile earnings after deducting estimated taxes, fuel, and mileage costs.
- **Milli AI**: conversational tool providing informational context about your financial data. Milli AI does not provide tax, legal, accounting, or investment advice.

### 4.2 Features in Development

The following features are currently in active development and are not yet fully available:

- **Financial Receipt**: per-payout documentation capturing payout amount, mileage, expenses, and reserve decisions.

### 4.3 Planned Features

The following features are planned but have not yet been built or released:
