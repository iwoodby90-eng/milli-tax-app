```
Milli Tax Vault™ is the tax operating system for gig workers, drivers, delivery pros, freelancers, and the self-employed.

Every time Uber, DoorDash, Instacart, Spark, Lyft, or any gig platform pays you, Milli's Autopilot™ instantly slices out the exact percentage you owe in taxes — federal, state, and self-employment — and moves it into your Milli Tax Vault™. No spreadsheets. No April panic. No IRS penalty for underpayment.

▸ MILLI AUTOPILOT™
  • Auto-slice every payout into your Tax Vault
  • Optional 401(k) + brokerage routing on the same trigger
  • Set a yearly goal — Milli protects the exact amount you need

▸ LIVE MILEAGE TRACKING
  • Background GPS detects business trips automatically
  • Every mile turns into a deduction at the current IRS rate
  • Never miss a $0.70/mi write-off again

▸ MILLI CENTS — OFFER PROFITABILITY ENGINE
  • Scores every Uber/DoorDash/Lyft/Spark offer in real time
  • Deducts gas, deadhead miles, and taxes to show your NET
  • Says GO, OKAY, or SKIP so you only take the offers that actually pay

▸ TAX READY SCORE™
  • Real-time gauge of how prepared you are for tax day
  • Live Financial Timeline of your Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4 payments
  • Vault Progress Story celebrates 25/50/75/100% milestones

▸ ELITE — IRS-READY FILING
  • One-tap Schedule C + SE PDF, filled from your data
  • Preparer-ready — attach in FreeTaxUSA, TurboTax, H&R Block, or hand to your CPA
  • Priority Milli AI for tax questions any time

▸ MILLI AI
  • On-device AI advisor trained on your numbers
  • "Am I on track for Q3?" "What deductions am I missing?"
  • Voice-enabled

▸ APPLE-QUALITY, EDGE-TO-EDGE
  • Native iOS design — carbon-fiber dark, cyan neon aurora, chrome accents
  • Real Google Maps mileage traces
  • Home Screen Widget shows your protected balance at a glance

Milli is a technology platform — not a bank, not a CPA. Your Tax Vault is held with our regulated banking partner under your name. Milli does not render tax opinions.
```
